    How to play

    1. Tap SPIN to start a trick.
    2. While the pen spins, the button waits. When the needle reaches the window it lights up: tap to link the next trick.
    3. Tap in the gold zone for PERFECT: it raises your multiplier. Too early and the pen drops.
    4. Stop tapping to bank the combo bonus: links² × 10 × multiplier.

    In Flow, when you tap (early · perfect · late) picks which connected trick comes next. Drag the hand to look around.

    Tap along
